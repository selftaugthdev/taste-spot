## About TasteSpot

TasteSpot is a daily geography game for people who love food and drink. Every
day brings five new questions — a dish, a drink, an ingredient, a technique —
and you tap the globe where it actually happened. No quizzes about country
flags, no trivia you half-remember: just "where was this actually made,
first?"

We fact-check every question before it goes live, and where an origin is
genuinely disputed, we say so and score generously across the region.

One round. Sixty seconds. Once a day.
