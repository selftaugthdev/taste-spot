## How to play

1. Read the clue — a dish, drink, or ingredient and a short description.
2. Tap the spot on the globe where you think it originated.
3. See your distance, your score, the real answer, and a fun fact.
4. Do that five times. Share your result.

A new round unlocks every day at your local midnight. You get one attempt per
day on the free tier — make it count.

### Scoring

Full details live on the "How scoring works" page, but in short: landing
inside the answer's region scores the full 1,000 points for that question.
Points decay smoothly the farther away your tap is, down toward zero past
about 5,000 km.
